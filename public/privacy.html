Privacy Policy

Electric Cats · last updated 26 July 2026

The short version. Electric Cats has no accounts, no logins, and no servers of its own. Your game is saved on your own device. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, which serves the optional reward videos — and it is configured to serve non-personalized ads, so it does not track you across apps.

What is stored, and where

Your progress — generators owned, research, credits, megaproject progress, settings — is written to your device's local storage by the app itself. It never leaves your device. We cannot read it, restore it, or transfer it. If you delete the app or clear its data, that save is gone permanently, which is why the game includes an Export button so you can keep your own copy.

Information we collect

None. Electric Cats does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal detail. There is no sign-up, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads — for example, to double the credits earned while you were away. Watching one is always your choice, and declining never blocks progress: if an ad cannot be shown for any reason, the game grants the bonus anyway.

These ads are delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob is configured to request non-personalized ads only, meaning ads are selected from general context rather than from a profile of your interests, and the app does not request your device's advertising identifier. Google may still process limited technical data needed to deliver and measure an ad — such as approximate region, device type, and whether the ad was viewed — and may do so as described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

Purchases

This version of Electric Cats contains no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in a future version, they will be handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play; we would never see or store your payment details, and this policy would be updated before that version ships.

Children

Electric Cats is not directed at children under 13, is not published in a children's category, and does not knowingly collect information from children. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to delete on request — but if you have a concern, please get in touch.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is no account to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. Your save data is under your direct control on your own device. To opt out of rewarded ads entirely, simply do not watch them. You can also reset the advertising settings on your device through your operating system's privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data is handled, the updated version will be posted here with a new date above, and the change will ship alongside the app update that requires it.
